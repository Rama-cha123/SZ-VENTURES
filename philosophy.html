Our Philosophy

We build. We don't advise.

A venture studio is structurally different from a consultancy. We take the risk, sit in the seat, and own the outcome — because the best way to understand a business is to run one.

The studio model, in plain terms.

Consultancies sell hours and recommendations. We sell outcomes. When we start a company, we put our own capital, time, and reputation behind it — the same way a founder does. That changes everything about how we work.

We're not here to produce a strategy deck and leave. We're here to recruit the team, ship the product, open the store, and answer the hard operational questions at 11pm on a Tuesday. The companies in our portfolio succeed or fail with us in the room.

“Advice is cheap. Conviction, capital, and a willingness to do the work — that's what moves a company from idea to institution.”

What this means in practice

  • Skin in the game. We invest our own capital alongside founders. Alignment is structural, not promised.
  • Operational depth. Hands-on support across recruiting, go-to-market, product, and finance — not slideware.
  • Long-term vision. An indefinite holding period. We compound value over decades, not quarters.
  • Ecosystem synergy. Shared infrastructure, talent, and insight across every company we run.
How we operate

Four principles, no exceptions.

Skin in the game

We invest our own capital alongside our founders. Every company has SZ Ventures as a committed, aligned partner — not an advisor collecting fees.

Operational depth

Our team has founded, scaled, and exited companies. We provide hands-on support — recruiting, go-to-market, product, finance — not strategic decks.

Long-term vision

We build for decades, not quarters. Our holding period is indefinite. We compound value by solving hard problems that require patience and conviction.

Ecosystem synergy

Our companies share infrastructure, talent, and insight. A breakthrough in manufacturing accelerates hospitality. The whole exceeds the parts.
