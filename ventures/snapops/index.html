Frequently Asked Questions

Is SnapOps an app I need to download?
No. SnapOps operates entirely through WhatsApp. There is nothing to download, no accounts to create, and no new interface to learn. You simply chat with our official WhatsApp channel to get what you need.
How are service providers onboarded?
Providers join through structured profile submission, skill verification, and local community references during each phased neighborhood launch.
How does payment work?
Payments are completely secure and handled directly within WhatsApp via UPI, Credit/Debit cards, or NetBanking. You only pay once the service is completed and you are satisfied.
