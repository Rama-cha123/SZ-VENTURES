Industrial Data Operations

High-performance AI models and autonomous robotic systems require immense volumes of physical, edge-case data that cannot be synthesized in simulated software environments. We build and manage the physical pipelines that collect this ground-truth data from actual industrial environments.

Workforce Deployment

Deploying specialized, trained field contributors to factories, fulfillment centers, transportation hubs, and outdoor spaces with specific data-gathering protocols and environmental calibration standards.

Data Collection

Capturing multimodal inputs including high-resolution video streams, LiDAR depth scans, spatial point clouds, and physical interaction recordings across varied lighting, weather, and physical conditions.

Quality Control

Rigorous multi-tier verification ensuring data integrity, accurate metadata labeling, sensor alignment, and strict adherence to client training formats before handoff.

Device Operations

Managing, maintaining, calibrating, and securely transferring specialized capture equipment, mobile sensory rigs, and edge recording hardware across field teams.
