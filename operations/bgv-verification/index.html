Physical Verification Capabilities

In an increasingly digital world, the most critical risk points still require physical confirmation. From confirming candidate residency to commercial business site verification, SZ Ventures deploys trained field personnel who capture geotagged, timestamped evidence directly on location.

Potential Services

  • Background Verification Support: Physical checks supplementing enterprise HR and hiring pipelines.
  • Address Verification: Confirmation of current and permanent residential addresses with neighbor validations where appropriate.
  • Physical Verification: Photographic and geolocation audit trails for commercial premises.
  • Document Collection: Physical retrieval and secure custody transfer of executed contracts, identity papers, and legal forms.
  • Field Validation & Site Verification: Independent validation of physical operational capacity for suppliers and merchant partners.
