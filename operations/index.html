OPERATING PROCESS

FROM REQUIREMENT TO FIELD EXECUTION

A seven-stage governance and delivery cycle ensuring every field task meets client specifications with verified audit trails.

01
Requirement

Client defines the task scope, location parameters, and SLAs.

02
Workforce

SZ sources suitable personnel aligned with task complexity.

03
Verification

Personnel are onboarded and verified as required for the assignment.

04
Deployment

Workers are assigned to locations, shift rosters, and tasks.

05
Execution

Work is performed according to defined standard operating procedures.

06
Quality Control

Evidence, geotags, and operational outputs are systematically reviewed.

07
Delivery

Validated results and structured operational data are delivered to the client.
