Field workforce for collecting, recovering, transferring and validating physical assets across distributed locations.

Asset Custody & Recovery Operations

Enterprise hardware, point-of-sale machines, leased consumer electronics, and telecom terminal devices often get stranded in the field when subscriptions lapse or personnel transition. SZ Ventures provides specialized field personnel who execute courteous, validated physical pickups with complete chains of custody.

Recovery Workflow

  • Device Collection: Coordination with end users to collect electronic units, peripherals, and diagnostic equipment.
  • Physical Condition Audit: Immediate photographic documentation of hardware serial numbers, cosmetic condition, and accessories.
  • Chain of Custody Validation: Digital acknowledgement signatures and tamper-evident packaging for return transport.
  • Reverse Logistics Transfer: Secure handoff to central logistics hubs, client service centers, or certified refurbishers.
