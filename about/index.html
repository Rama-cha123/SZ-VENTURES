SZ VENTURES

BUILDING BUSINESSES. OPERATING IN THE REAL WORLD.

SZ Ventures is a venture-building and operating company focused on businesses where technology, people and physical execution intersect. We identify opportunities, build the operating infrastructure, put businesses into the field and scale what works.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

IDENTIFY → BUILD → OPERATE → SCALE

Find opportunities where real-world problems create room for new businesses.

03

04

FOUNDATIONAL VALUES

WHAT WE BELIEVE

REAL PROBLEMS FIRST

We build around problems that exist in the real world. Real customer demand, physical operational challenges, and genuine economic friction take precedence over abstract concepts.

EXECUTION MATTERS

A business is not built by ideas alone. Enduring competitive moats are constructed through operational competence, field reliability, and disciplined day-to-day execution.

PEOPLE + TECHNOLOGY

Technology becomes more powerful when paired with capable people and operational infrastructure. Software coordinates; people execute and deliver value in the physical world.

BUILD TO OPERATE

We don't only create concepts. We build systems that can actually run. Every model is engineered for real workflows, clear accountability, and sustainable economics.

FOUNDER

THE FOUNDER BEHIND SZ VENTURES.

Sridhar Chakkravarthy

Founder — SZ Ventures Private Limited

Sridhar Chakkravarthy is an entrepreneur and the Founder of SZ Ventures Private Limited. His professional journey includes experience with UPS, InterGlobe Technologies (IndiGo), and IBM, where he developed expertise across global operations, aviation, technology, process excellence, and enterprise systems.

His international exposure across India, Europe, Singapore, the UAE, the United States, and the United Kingdom has shaped his global perspective on business, culture, and emerging markets.

Transitioning from corporate enterprise to venture building, Sridhar now applies this experience to developing practical, technology-led businesses across industrial technology and the new economy. His approach combines operational discipline, market understanding, and long-term company building.

He holds an MBA from Warwick Business School and is a certified Six Sigma Master Black Belt.
