SnapOps

Intelligent operations software that runs the back office of modern hospitality and retail — scheduling, inventory, and forecasting, automated.

Founded 2023 Growth stage 2,000+ locations live
Overview

The operating system for multi-location businesses.

Most operators run their business on spreadsheets, gut feel, and 11pm text messages. SnapOps replaces that with a single system that learns how each location actually works — then quietly handles the planning, ordering, and forecasting that used to eat the day.

It started inside Bowl & Sip Co., where we needed to run forty stores without drowning in admin. We built the tool we wished existed, used it to scale the brand, and now it powers thousands of locations far beyond our own walls.
