01 — Studio
Operating Model

A studio, not a fund.

We embed as co-founders, not bystanders — and we stay until the company stands on its own.

01

Conviction over consensus

We back sharp theses and commit fully. Few bets, held with intensity, compounded over a decade.

02

Operators, not observers

We recruit the team, ship the first product, and run the company until it earns its independence.

03

Capital with craft

Patient, structured capital paired with the design, brand, and engineering discipline of a studio.

04

Built to endure

We optimise for permanence — durable moats, real margins, and companies that outlive the cycle.
