Our Philosophy

Execution over advice.
Building over consulting.

A venture studio is structurally different from a consultancy. We take the risk, sit in the seat, and own the outcome.

Skin in the game

We invest our own capital alongside our founders. Every company in our portfolio has SZ Ventures as a committed, aligned partner — not an advisor collecting fees.

Operational depth

Our team has founded, scaled, and exited companies. We provide hands-on support — recruiting, go-to-market, product, finance — not strategic decks.

Long-term vision

We build for decades, not quarters. Our holding period is indefinite. We compound value by solving hard problems that require patience and conviction.

Ecosystem synergy

Our companies share infrastructure, talent, and insight. A breakthrough in manufacturing accelerates hospitality. AI in operations powers sustainability. The whole exceeds the parts.
