Overview

The hard problems worth solving.

Software scaled fast because it was cheap to iterate. The physical world didn't — because building hardware and controls is slow, capital-intensive, and unforgiving. Mirai exists to close that gap inside the studio.

We prototype relentlessly, ship working systems to pilot partners, and feed everything we learn back into the ecosystem — powering SnapOps at the edge, automating SZ Manufacturing, and sensing the world for TerraMuse.
