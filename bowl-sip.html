Bowl & Sip Co.

A fast-casual brand rethinking everyday food — nourishing bowls and craft beverages, built on a profitable unit model designed to scale.

Founded 2022 40 stores 3 regions
Overview

Food that respects your day, and your body.

Bowl & Sip started with a simple frustration: the food you can grab on a busy day is rarely the food you actually want to eat. We built a brand around the bowl — flexible, fresh, and fast — paired with beverages people genuinely look forward to.

Underneath the warmth is a serious operating machine. Every store runs on SnapOps, sources through a shared supply chain, and feeds real-world demand data back into the studio. It's hospitality with a venture-studio engine behind it.
